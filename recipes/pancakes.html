🥞 Ultimate Fluffy Homemade Pancakes

Prep Time: 10 mins | Cook Time: 15 mins | Yield: 8–10 pancakes

Difficulty: Easy | Cuisine: American Breakfast

About This Recipe

Say goodbye to boxed mixes! These homemade pancakes are thick, soft, melt-in-your-mouth tender, and ridiculously easy to make from scratch. Using everyday pantry ingredients, this recipe produces golden-brown pancakes with tall, cloud-like centers every single time.

Ingredients

Dry Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp fine sea salt

Wet Ingredients

  • 1½ cups milk (or buttermilk for extra tang)
  • 1 large egg, room temperature
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter, melted and slightly cooled
  • 1 tsp pure vanilla extract

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Whisk the Dry Ingredients: In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  2. Combine the Wet Ingredients: In a separate bowl or measuring cup, whisk the milk, egg, melted butter, and vanilla extract until well combined.
  3. Mix the Batter (Do Not Overmix!): Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients. Gently fold with a spatula just until combined. Lumps are completely fine—overmixing creates dense pancakes! Let the batter rest for 5 minutes while preheating your griddle.
  4. Preheat & Grease: Heat a non-stick skillet or griddle over medium-low heat. Brush lightly with butter or neutral oil.
  5. Cook to Golden Perfection: Pour ¼ cup of batter per pancake onto the hot surface. Cook until bubbles form on the surface and the edges look set (2 to 3 minutes). Flip gently and cook the other side for another 1 to 2 minutes until golden.
  6. Serve Hot: Stack high with warm maple syrup, a pat of salted butter, and fresh berries or chocolate chips.

💡 Chef's Secret: Letting the batter rest for 5–10 minutes allows the flour to hydrate and the baking powder to activate early, giving you extra tall and fluffy layers!
