Slow Cooked Lasagna

Description

This vibrant and wholesome dish brings together fresh ingredients to create a perfect balance of textures and flavors. It is quick to prepare, incredibly satisfying, and makes for a fantastic lunch or dinner option any day of the week.

Whether you're cooking for yourself or hosting family and friends, this recipe is designed to be simple yet impressive, delivering restaurant-quality results right from your own kitchen.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups fresh mixed greens or spinach
  • 1 ripe avocado, sliced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 cup cucumber, diced
  • 1 grilled chicken breast or plant-based alternative
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Wash and thoroughly dry the mixed greens, then place them in a large mixing bowl.
  2. Prepare the vegetables by halving the cherry tomatoes, slicing the avocado, and dicing the cucumber.
  3. Add the tomatoes, cucumber, and avocado slices on top of the bed of greens.
  4. Slice the cooked chicken breast and arrange it neatly over the salad mixture.
  5. In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper to make the dressing.
  6. Drizzle the dressing evenly over the dish just before serving, toss gently, and enjoy!
