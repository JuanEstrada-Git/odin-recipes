🇨🇴 Authentic Bandeja Paisa

Prep Time: 20 mins | Cook Time: 2 hrs | Servings: 4

Difficulty: Medium | Cuisine: Colombian (Antioquia)

About This Dish

The iconic national dish of Colombia, Bandeja Paisa, is a hearty, mountain-style platter originating from the Paisa region. Originally crafted to provide field workers with enough energy to sustain a full day of heavy labor, this feast brings together a vibrant variety of savory flavors and rich textures—from smoky beans and crispy chicharrón to tender carne molida and sweet plantains.

Ingredients

The Staples

  • 2 cups cooked white rice
  • 4 fried eggs (sunny-side up)
  • 2 ripe avocados, halved
  • 4 small corn arepas

The Sides

  • Sweet Plantains (Tajadas): 2 ripe yellow plantains, sliced diagonally and fried until caramelized.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prepare the Beans & Rice: Simmer the beans with garlic, onion, cumin, and pork seasonings until thick and creamy. Keep warm alongside fresh steamed white rice.
  2. Crisp the Chicharrón: Score the pork belly into classic ribbon strips. Fry over medium heat until the skin turns blistered, crunchy, and deep golden brown.
  3. Cook the Meats: Sauté the ground beef with savory hogao until fully cooked and tender. Pan-fry the chorizos (and morcilla, if using) until well-browned.
  4. Fry Plantains & Eggs: Fry sweet plantain slices until soft and caramelized. In a separate pan, cook eggs sunny-side up with runny yolks.
  5. Assemble the Platter: Arrange generous portions on large oval platters. Place the rice and beans in the center, framing them with the crispy chicharrón, chorizo, seasoned beef, sweet plantains, sliced avocado, and warm arepa. Top the rice with a fried egg.

💡 Pro-Tip: Serve with a side of homemade hogao (Colombian tomato-onion creole sauce) and fresh lime wedges to cut through the rich flavors!
